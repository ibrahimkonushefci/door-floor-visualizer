# hallway-render-02 — Blender-rendered room (hallway)

Built by `tools/blender/build_room.py --room hallway-render-02` (room module `tools/blender/rooms/hallway_render_02.py`) and rendered by `tools/blender/render_layers.py` in Blender 5.2.2 LTS (Cycles, Apple M4 (GPU - 10 cores)), 512 samples per pass, seed 1729, adaptive sampling off, OpenImageDenoise. Imported by `scripts/import-rendered-scene.ts`. Exact settings, camera matrix and render times: `render-manifest.json`.

**Geometry (true scale).** Entrance hall 2.4 × 5.0 × 2.7 m, no window (linear ceiling light); floating oak console, oak slat panel, shoe cabinet. Door leaf 900 × 2000 × 40 mm in a 1000 × 2050 mm rough opening; casing 70 × 16 mm, skirting 80 × 16 mm (products `pervaze-mdf`, `lajsne-mdf`). Camera 19 mm on a 36 mm sensor, level, lens shift -0.14000000059604645. Floor, door and trim mappings are exact projections of the modelled planes, so plank size is physically correct.

**Layers.** Masks are shader-AOV coverage (antialiased), jointly quantised so editable coverage never exceeds 255 per pixel. Light plates are Cycles diffuse light (Combined of a 50 % grey Lambertian pass ÷ 0.5), so contact shadows and bounce light are real; their constant light colour is stored as `tint`. Reflection plates are a black-dielectric (F0 0.04) pass, scaled at runtime by each material's gloss. The base plate is the light pass with each editable surface's share removed (exact at edges from a second pass at 25 % grey). `original.png` is the neutral render used by compare-with-original. `floor-position.png` is 16-bit world X/Y in 0.1 mm units (origin -300, -300 mm).

**Checks at render time.** Position map vs Cycles Position pass: median 0.002 mm, p99 0.007 mm; one sample every 4 px, bilinear reconstruction vs the exact plane max 0.160 mm (p99 0.094 mm, includes quantisation). Base edge clamp: p99 0.0403, max 0.0579 (linear). Hidden surfaces (not exported): none.

**Door models.** filomuro, flat, grooved, glass-insert (`tools/blender/room_spec.py` DOOR_MODELS), same camera, seed and samples (`render_layers.py --model`), encoded with the filomuro light scale. The scene's own layers are filomuro; `model-<id>-*.png` are every model's layers cropped to one shared region (956, 230, 634, 910 px), pasted over the default layers at load time. The other models were rendered only inside a border around the door (`render_layers.py --crop`; Cycles samples each pixel from its own coordinates, so those pixels equal a full render). Region method, border differences and render times: `door-models.json`. Filomuro is flush with the wall in a concealed aluminium frame with hidden hinges and no casing; its skirting runs to the opening. The glass insert is satin glass lit from a short corridor behind the door.

**Inputs.** CC0 models, textures and HDRI from Poly Haven used by this room are listed with `usedIn` in `tools/blender/assets.json` (hashes in `tools/blender/assets.lock.json`, ledger `renderInputs`). Everything else (shell, door assembly, procedural furniture, materials) is modelled in `tools/blender/{build_room,props}.py` and the room module.
