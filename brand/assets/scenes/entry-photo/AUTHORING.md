# entry-photo — real photograph

Source: Alex Tyson (Unsplash @alextyson195), https://unsplash.com/photos/an-empty-room-with-white-doors-and-wooden-floors-K2qofQMdr6w. Unsplash License: free to use commercially and non-commercially, no permission or attribution required. Real photograph (Nikon Z 6), not AI-generated.

Downloaded at 2400×1600 (JPEG, q92), cropped to 0, 0, 2133, 1600 and resized to 1600×1200 (`base.png`, frozen). All other layers are derived by `scripts/author-photo-scene.mjs`.

Camera model: horizon y=600, vanishing points 3046,600 (door wall) and 462,600 (corridor wall), focal 871.3 px, camera height 830 mm from a 2000 mm leaf. Leaf width 917 mm.

Lighting: painted trim and leaf use the photograph's luminance; the oak floor uses a mask-normalised low-pass (σ 22 px) so the original planks do not print through new floors. The second door, the open door at left and the foreground jamb remain photographic.
