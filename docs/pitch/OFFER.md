## Owner's notes (do not send)

Decisions to make before this goes out:

1. **Model:** one-off setup only, setup + subscription, or subscription only. Hosting has a running cost only once the site is deployed (no deployment yet).
2. **Amounts and currency** (Kosovo uses EUR), **trial** length and whether it is paid, **payment terms**, **offer validity**.
3. **What "setup" includes:** how many products and décors, whether new furnished rooms are included or extra (on this Mac a new room renders in roughly 15–30 minutes for its default door model plus 3–9 minutes for each other model, on top of the modelling time).
4. **Not in the offer on purpose:** the own-room photo upload. Its AI model's commercial-use licence is unresolved (see [README licensing reminder](../../README.md#licensing-reminder)); add it only after that is reviewed or the model is replaced.
5. **Décor permission:** the demo uses Kronotex décor images without a reuse licence. A customer version needs the showroom's or supplier's written permission, which is why the offer asks for it.
6. **Production acceptance before go-live:** test on a real iPad and iPhone ([../plan/IPAD_BASELINE.md](../plan/IPAD_BASELINE.md)), switch `demoMode` off with a real HTTPS address and verified contacts, and deploy — only when you decide.
