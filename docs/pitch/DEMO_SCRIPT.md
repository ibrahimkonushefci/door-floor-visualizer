# 3-minute demo script

For the person presenting to a showroom owner or importer. Laptop first (1440×900 or larger); the same steps work at iPad size. Albanian lines are what to say; English is for you. Everything shown is **demo content** under the "Porta & Dysheme" demo brand.

Before you start, go through the checklist in [README.md](README.md#before-every-demo).

## The one idea to land

> **"Klienti juaj e sheh derën dhe dyshemenë në një dhomë të vërtetë para se të blejë — dhe ju e merrni kërkesën në WhatsApp me kombinimin e saktë."**
> Your customer sees the door and floor in a real-looking room before buying — and you get the request on WhatsApp with the exact combination.

## The script

The presenter sheet (press and hold the logo for about a second, or add `?presenter=1` to the address) lists **3-minute demo** with four stops. Each tap opens that stop's room and combination. Stops 1–4 below match it.

| Time | Do | They see | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | Stop **1 · Living room — start** is already open (or tap it). Optional: **Present full screen** (top-left of the picture). | A furnished living room: white door, natural oak floor (Kronotex Premium Eiche Natur). | "Sot klienti shfleton katalogë PDF dhe mostra të vogla, dhe duhet ta imagjinojë vetë. Këtu e sheh në dhomë." *(Today customers flip through PDFs and small samples and have to imagine it. Here they see it in a room.)* |
| **0:20–1:00** | Tap the **floor** in the picture → the floor choices open. Tap two or three floors (e.g. Norfolk Oak Kaffee, then Everest Oak Weiß). Switch **Plank direction** to Crosswise. Klienti nuk ka më frikë se 'a shkon kjo me dyshemenë'." *(Door, frame and skirting together. The customer stops worrying whether it goes with the floor.)* |

Funksionon edhe pa internet në sallon. A doni ta provojmë me produktet tuaja?" *(All of this with your logo, your products and your number — in Albanian, English and German for the diaspora. **Closing ask:** a short pilot with their logo, contacts and their own décors ([OFFER.md](OFFER.md)). Do not quote prices until the owner has set them.

## If they want more (after the 3 minutes)

- **Room filter and the bathroom:** chips above the room thumbnails. In the bathroom only the door changes ("Tiles – laminate does not go into bathrooms").
- **Compare with the original room:** the button on the picture with the slider — shows what the render adds.
- **Catalog:** the **Catalog** link: product grid, filters, product pages with "See it in a room".
- **Offline:** presenter sheet → **Save for offline use** (about 31 MB, done before the meeting). Turn Wi-Fi off and keep going.
- **Languages:** the language menu (SQ / EN / DE) — useful for diaspora customers.
- **Own-room photo ("Try it in your own room"):** **do not show this to a prospective customer yet.** The photo tool's AI model has an unresolved commercial-use licence ([../../README.md](../../README.md#licensing-reminder)). If asked, say: "A version where the customer uploads a photo of their own room is in development."

## Recovery

- **A room takes a few seconds the first time:** normal on first open; keep talking. Later visits are faster. Saving offline beforehand helps.
- **Lost or something looks odd:** presenter sheet → tap the stop again, or press **Reset** on the picture.
- **Download or A/B is greyed out:** the picture is still finishing; wait a moment.
- **The QR code shows "This link points to this computer":** expected from a local preview. Say that on the live site it opens on the customer's phone, or use the LAN preview ([README.md](README.md#showing-the-qr-code-on-a-real-phone)).

## Likely questions — honest answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Are these my products?" | "Not yet — this is a demo with real Kronotex décors. For you we load your doors, floors and trims." |
| "How accurate are the colours?" | "Illustrative — the final colour is always chosen from the physical sample. The disclaimer is on every screen." |
| "Can my customers use it at home?" | "Yes — through a link or the QR code, on the phone. In demo mode nothing is sent; live, the request comes to your WhatsApp or Viber." |
| "Does it work on an iPad?" | "It is built for iPad and phone sizes. Final testing on your own device is part of setup." (Physical iPad/iPhone testing has not been done yet.) |
| "Can it use a photo of the customer's room?" | "In development." (See the licence note above. Do not demonstrate or promise it.) |
| "How much does it cost?" | "That depends on the number of products and rooms; I'll send you an offer." (Prices: owner decision, [OFFER.md](OFFER.md).) |
| "Who else uses it?" | Answer truthfully; do not name clients you do not have. |

## Do not say

- That the décor images or the photo AI are licensed for commercial use — neither is yet.
- That it has been tested on real iPads/iPhones — it has only been tested in a desktop browser at those sizes.
- That prices, timelines or delivery dates are fixed — they are the owner's decision.
